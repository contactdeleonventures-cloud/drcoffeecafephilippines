Our story

First sketched on a flight.

Dr. Coffee started with a doctor who loved coffee. The concept and logo were drawn on a flight from Korea to the Philippines, every recipe was tested for months, and a small kiosk grew into a clinic-inspired café where sizes became doses.

Good to know

Before you visit.

A little planning. More room to enjoy.

Where are the spaces?

The three private rooms are at Cebu Main: 2F Jaeja Building, corner J. Llorente Street and Osmeña Boulevard, Cebu City. Individual work stations are at Casa Mira in Labangon. See branch photos and directions →

When can I visit?

Cebu Main’s usual café hours are 7 AM–1 AM the next day; Casa Mira is usually open 24 hours. Ask the branch about special dates and your preferred room or station time. Café hours do not guarantee room availability or overnight booking access.

What does the private-room price include?

Each listed Cebu Main package covers four hours. Conference Room includes ₱1,000 worth of food and drinks; Doctor’s Lounge includes ₱2,000; Portal Space includes ₱5,000. These are the included food-and-drink values, not the full room fee. Equipment and amenities are listed separately on each card.

How do I reserve a room?

Copy the booking format above and send it to the branch that handles your room or pass. The branch checks availability before giving payment instructions. The standard down payment is ₱500 for any one of the three Cebu Main rooms. The branch verifies payment and rechecks the schedule before confirming. Please wait for those instructions before paying.

Can I extend my stay or book a different duration?

Ask the branch for availability and a quote. The listed prices are four-hour packages; they do not establish hourly or overtime rates. See the Casa Mira pass rates above. Ask the branch about extensions or access under the Weekly Plan.

What if I need to change or cancel?

Keep changes and cancellations in the same branch conversation. Ask the branch to confirm its current balance, cancellation and refund conditions before payment.

Can you help with a custom setup or larger group?

Email asvcphilcorporation@gmail.com for custom packages and questions outside the branch’s standard offer. Admin replies are handled Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM Philippine time.
